# EXALTED — Article content sheet

Complete one copy of the submission table for each article. Supply the artwork as a separate file. Use the exact wording, punctuation, and author credit you want readers to see.

## Fields we use

| Field | What to provide | Required? | Current display behavior |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Vitrine / article number | Assigned slot, 01–08. | Assigned by editor | Numbering runs left–right from the entrance: 01–02, 03–04, 05–06, 07–08. Previous / Next follows this order. |
| Category | Short label, such as GAMES, ESPORTS, or LIFE. | Yes | Appears above the title, together with the article number. |
| Title | Article headline. | Yes | Main heading in the article window. |
| Subtitle | Optional supporting line below the title. | Optional | Appears below the title and above the author credit. Leave blank to hide it. |
| Author / byline | Exact credit, including “By” if desired. A date may be included in the same line. | Optional | Appears below the subtitle, or directly below the title when there is no subtitle. There is no separate date field. |
| Lead / preview | Short introduction visible before Read more. | Optional for a long article | A supplied preview replaces the first body paragraph in the collapsed view. Otherwise, the first body paragraph is shown. |
| Article text | Full article, divided into paragraphs. For a short caption, provide just one paragraph. | Yes | Read more reveals the complete body. A separate preview is replaced by the body when expanded; it is not automatically added to the body. |
| Bold / italic | Preserve the intended bold and italic words in the supplied document. | Optional | Both are supported in article body text. Title, subtitle, byline, and a separate preview are plain text. |
| Artwork / image | Exact filename and the image file, preferably WebP, as currently used. | Yes for supplied artwork | The same image appears in the 3D vitrine and article window. The complete image keeps its proportions; the vitrine size stays fixed. |
| Read more | State Yes for a longer article or No for a short caption. | Yes, as an editorial choice | Currently automatic: more than one body paragraph shows Read more; one paragraph hides it. The button disappears after expansion. Other combinations need an implementation change. |
| Zoom image | State Yes or No. | Yes, as an editorial choice | Currently enabled for Halloween only. Clicking its image opens a screen-height view with proportions preserved. Other articles need zoom enabled in the implementation. |

Display order: **Category / number → Title → Subtitle (if supplied) → Author (if supplied) → Lead or first paragraph → Read more (if applicable).**
